REPKIT Privacy Policy

Effective 5 October 2026

REPKIT is an interval timer and rep counter for Android, made by David Mitenko, an individual developer. This policy explains what data the app handles and what leaves your phone.

Your workout data stays on your phone

Your entries, reps, totals, streaks, holds, timer settings, check-in history and workout sessions are stored only on your device. REPKIT has no account, no sign-in and no server of its own, and the developer cannot see this data.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include REPKIT's data in your device backup. That backup is handled by Google under your account's settings, and the developer has no access to it.

Crash reports and usage statistics

To find and fix bugs, REPKIT uses two Google services, Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase. While the setting Share crash reports and usage is on, they send the following:

  • Crash reports: the error and where in the app it happened, the device model, the Android version, the app version, a random installation ID, and short technical notes about what the app was doing (for example, that it repaired an invalid setting).
  • Usage statistics: standard events such as the app being opened, the length of a session and the screens viewed. They come with a random app instance ID, the device model, the Android version, the language and an approximate country, which Google works out from the connection.

Never sent: your workout data, entry names or check-in history. REPKIT records no custom events about your workouts.

This data is processed by Google on the developer's behalf; see Firebase privacy and security. Crash reports are kept for 90 days. Usage data is kept for Google Analytics' standard period, no more than 14 months.

Your choice

The setting is on by default. To turn it off, open REPKIT, tap ⚙ Settings on the main screen, and switch off Share crash reports and usage. Both services stop at once, and any crash reports that haven't been sent yet are deleted.

What REPKIT doesn't do

  • It shows no ads.
  • It does not sell or share your data for advertising.
  • It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

The permissions it does use (vibration, notifications, wake lock and a foreground service) keep the timer running and cueing you while the screen is off.

Deleting your data

To delete everything, clear REPKIT's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Inside the app, Reset progress can also clear an entry's history. Data already sent to Firebase can't be linked to you, because it carries only random IDs; it is removed after the retention periods above.

Children

REPKIT is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. A change in what is collected will also be noted in the app's release notes.
